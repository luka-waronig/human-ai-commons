# Human-AI Commons research studies

Human-AI Commons studies coupled strategic choices and local renewable-resource stocks in a stylized spatial system. Human and AI-infrastructure agents have different extraction intensities and share an environment-dependent binary decision rule.

## ICCS 2026 study

Ivana Malčić, Debraj Roy, and Luka Waronig. *Emergent Spatial Traps in the Coevolutionary Commons: Human-AI Cooperation Under Environmental Feedback*. Computational Science – ICCS 2026 Workshops, pages 95–109. [Published chapter](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29909-3_8). [Supplementary information](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18713008).

The chapter examines high cooperation alongside stressed environmental states and spatially structured resource–strategy dynamics.

The reference Weitz replicator ODE and the implemented noisy best-response ABM use different transition laws. A difference between their equilibria cannot be attributed solely to spatial structure or stochasticity.

## Journal extension

The Journal of Computational Science extension has a [self-contained study folder](../jcs/docs/running-experiments.md). [The experiments of the paper](../jcs/docs/experiments.md) test whether the depleted locations of the conference study persist and what determines them, search the payoff family that contains the original game for changes that serve human and AI agents together, divide the unmet demand by what limits its delivery, and test three local interventions applied to AI agents: scarcity pricing, an extraction cap and capacity-based siting. The study includes an independent model, an experiment runner, a dependency lock and tests.

The folder also keeps an earlier staged payoff search, an exploratory [research protocol](../jcs/docs/research-plan.md) for constrained Pareto payoff design that preceded the experiments of the paper. It is not reported in the paper; two payoff designs it selected appear among the decision rules of the persistence experiment.
