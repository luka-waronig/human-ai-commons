# Human-AI Commons research studies

Human-AI Commons studies coupled strategic choices and local renewable-resource stocks in a stylized spatial system. Human and AI-infrastructure agents have different extraction intensities and share an environment-dependent binary decision rule.

## ICCS 2026 study

Ivana Malčić, Debraj Roy, and Luka Waronig. *Emergent Spatial Traps in the Coevolutionary Commons: Human-AI Cooperation Under Environmental Feedback*. Computational Science – ICCS 2026 Workshops, pages 95–109. [Published chapter](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29909-3_8). [Supplementary information](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18713008).

The chapter examines high cooperation alongside stressed environmental states and spatially structured resource–strategy dynamics.

The reference Weitz replicator ODE and the implemented noisy best-response ABM use different transition laws. A difference between their equilibria cannot be attributed solely to spatial structure or stochasticity.

## Journal extension

The Journal of Computational Science extension has a [self-contained study folder](../jcs/docs/running-experiments.md) and a [research protocol](../jcs/docs/research-plan.md) covering constrained Pareto payoff design for human support, AI support, and local depletion. The main study estimates their trade-offs while restricting persistent local resource stress. Matched resource-only controls and human-only/AI-only objective comparisons test the role of strategic information and social priorities. The study includes an independent model, staged experiment runner, dependency lock, tests, and replay bundles. The [numerical configuration](../jcs/configs/research_protocol.json) specifies the experiments.
